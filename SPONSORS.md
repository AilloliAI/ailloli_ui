# Sponsoring Ailloli UI

GitHub Sponsors offers a voluntary way to sustain open-source maintenance,
bug fixes, performance, documentation, features, and long-term stability.

**Sponsorship funds the development of Ailloli UI; it does not purchase the
right to use it.** Individual, commercial, and open-source use permitted by the
Apache-2.0/MIT license never requires sponsorship.

Sponsorship is separate from any future professional support, integration,
training, service-level agreement, or custom development contract. Sponsors do
not receive private-repository access, advance features, guaranteed support
time, bug priority, roadmap control, or technical decision rights.

## Proposed monthly tiers

The following tiers are proposals until they are explicitly approved and
published on [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/AilloliAI):

- **Supporter — 5 USD/month:** GitHub badge and optional listing in Supporters.
- **Backer — 25 USD/month:** optional name or handle with a link.
- **Bronze Sponsor — 100 USD/month:** optional organization name and link.
- **Silver Sponsor — 250 USD/month:** optional local logo up to 120 px wide in
  this file.
- **Gold Sponsor — 500 USD/month:** optional local logo up to 180 px wide in the
  README, this file, and a future documentation Sponsors page.
- **Corporate Sponsor — 1,000 USD/month:** optional local logo up to 240 px wide
  in major Sponsors locations.

Recognition is optional and requires explicit consent. Anonymous sponsorship
is always accepted. Public entries are alphabetical within a tier, and all
sponsors in the same tier receive the same recognition rules.

## Recognition and asset policy

No sponsor is displayed before consent and verification. A sponsor may ask to
change or remove its public recognition at any time. Private sponsors are not
disclosed.

Logos must be static, non-animated files stored locally in the repository. No
remote image, tracking pixel, analytics parameter, or sponsor-controlled embed
is allowed. Before adding a logo, maintainers record its source, usage
permission, license or trademark terms, dimensions, and SHA-256 in the public
asset manifest. Recognition is descriptive, not an endorsement or testimonial.

## Operator checklist

- Confirm that the beneficiary is exactly the `AilloliAI` organization and the
  public Sponsors URL does not redirect to another beneficiary.
- Keep eligibility, identity, banking, tax, and fiscal-host data outside Git,
  commands, logs, issues, and build artifacts.
- Prepare all six monthly tiers as drafts and obtain explicit financial
  approval before publishing them.
- GitHub permits up to ten monthly tiers. A published tier's price cannot be
  edited; changing it requires retiring the tier and creating a new one.
- Never create a tier that promises private access, a service-level agreement,
  support time, bug priority, custom development, exclusive functionality, or
  influence over the roadmap.
- Publish `.github/FUNDING.yml` only after the organization profile is publicly
  active and independently verified.
- Do not configure sponsorship webhooks or automated financial-data exports.

## FAQ

### Is sponsorship required to use Ailloli UI?

No. The license governs use of the framework, and sponsorship is voluntary.

### Does sponsorship make a request more likely to be implemented?

No. Technical decisions consider project scope, safety, maintainability,
community value, and available capacity—not sponsor status.

### Can a sponsor remain anonymous?

Yes. Recognition is opt-in, and private sponsorship is never disclosed.

### Can an organization purchase support through a tier?

No. Future professional services would use a separate written agreement with
its own scope and terms.
